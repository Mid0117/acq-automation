The Frame

The Plan of Record is the one place Adam, Kebrina, and the build team look to answer: "what are we doing, who's doing it, when does it land?" Every workstream below ties to one of the four pillars in the master roadmap (Blake voice agent, listing pipeline, vault + self-improvement, training videos) and one of the build asks from the 2026-06-03 strategy call.

Posture. Real-time, event-driven, no cron. Webhooks + Cloudflare Workers. The only periodic thing is the prompt-iteration loop, and even that fires on transcript volume, not the calendar.

From the 2026-06-03 call — Adam's build asks (treat as spec)
  1. Appointment drill-down — click "36 appointments in contract" → view the individual profiles behind the number.
  2. Claude-assessed conversation ratings — individual metrics derived from the actual call audio, not a manual score.
  3. Success-rate-vs-target display — e.g. are we hitting 70% of the success measure for a given task.
  4. Calendar view preferred over a plain column view (to confirm).
